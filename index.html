How the app turns rounds into an estimate

Handi keeps your round history, compares scores using the method you choose, and shows which recent results contribute to your estimate. It is a personal tracking tool; an official Handicap Index is maintained through an authorized golf association or club.

Estimate, not an official index. The app uses your entered gross score and does not calculate every World Handicap System adjustment or safeguard.

How the estimate is built

  1. 1
    Record a round

    Only rounds marked as qualifying enter the handicap window.

  2. 2
    Compare its value

    Over Par mode uses gross score minus par. Rating + Slope mode converts the entered gross score to an estimated differential.

  3. 3
    Use the lower values

    The default score-count schedule selects the lowest available values from your latest qualifying-round window, then averages them.

  4. 4
    Let the window roll

    Each new qualifying round moves the window forward. The oldest round in that window leaves first; non-qualifying rounds do not move it.

Course Rating and Slope

Course Rating is the expected score for a scratch golfer on a particular set of tees under normal conditions. Slope Rating describes how much harder those tees play for a bogey golfer relative to a scratch golfer. A slope of 113 is the standard reference point.

App differential = (gross score - Course Rating) x 113 / Slope Rating

For example, a gross 90 on tees rated 72.0 with a slope of 120 gives an app differential of 17.0. The official WHS calculation starts from an adjusted gross score and may include a Playing Conditions Calculation (PCC).

Custom score selection

Turn on Use custom best X of Y in Handicap Forecast to choose how many of your lowest values count and how many recent qualifying rounds form the window. For example, best 5 of latest 12 uses up to five lowest available values from those 12 qualifying rounds.

Custom selection replaces the default count schedule and does not apply the early-record adjustment. Rounds without a saved course profile do not provide a differential while Rating + Slope mode is active.

What the app leaves out

  • Hole-by-hole net double bogey limits used to create an adjusted gross score.
  • The daily Playing Conditions Calculation.
  • Low Handicap Index caps, exceptional-score reductions, and committee adjustments.
  • Course Handicap or Playing Handicap for a specific game or format.

These details can make an official Handicap Index differ from the app's estimate.

Entering course values and saving data

Courses are shared. When you add a course, everyone using the app can find it and use the same Course Rating and Slope Rating, so check the Courses page before creating a duplicate. If a value looks wrong for you, enter your own: your values apply only to your account, and to every round you have at that course.

Enter Course Rating and Slope Rating for the exact tees you played. If you use multiple tee sets at the same venue, add each set as its own course, for example “Royal Durban – White tees”.

Your rounds, courses and settings are saved to your account, so they are there whenever you sign in. Export and import under Your Data remain available as backups.
